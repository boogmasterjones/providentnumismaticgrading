## 1. Process claims to confirm (standard practice unless marked "their site")

These are on **/process** (and summarised on the home page, /submit and in the FAQ). Each needs a yes, a correction or
removal.

**Receipt and intake**
- [ ] Each package is opened and checked against its submission (number of coins, descriptions, service requested). *Standard practice.*
- [ ] Each coin is logged individually so it can be traced through every step. *Standard practice.*
- [ ] If anything does not match the submission, we contact the customer before work begins. *Standard practice.*

**Authentication**
- [ ] The coin's weight, dimensions, style, fabric and manufacturing marks are compared with what is known for the type. *Standard practice.*
- [ ] A specific gravity test can be carried out (their site lists it as a service) and does not harm the coin (*standard*).
- [ ] A coin that cannot be authenticated is not encapsulated. *Standard practice.* What happens to it (returned ungraded? any note or fee?) is not said anywhere.

**Attribution**
- [ ] Identified by issuer or authority, ruler, denomination, date or period, mint where it can be determined, and variety or standard catalogue reference. *Standard practice.*
- [ ] Ancient coins at the services that include it get expanded identification and a recorded weight. *Their site* (Ancient Standard and Premiere).
- [ ] A pedigree can be noted on the label on request. *Their site* (listed as an extra).

**Grading**
- [ ] Wear, strike, surfaces, luster and eye appeal are judged together. *Standard practice.*
- [ ] Milled coins are graded on the 1 to 70 scale. *Not stated on their site.*
- [ ] Ancient coins get a descriptive grade with separate strike and surface subgrades. *Their site mentions subgrades; a photo of their label shows "AU, Str: 5/5, Srf: 5/5".* Confirm the 1 to 5 scale.
- [ ] Problems such as cleaning or damage are noted rather than hidden (details grades). *Standard practice.*

**Quality control**
- [ ] Before sealing, the attribution, grade and every label detail are reviewed again against the coin. *Standard practice.* Who does the review (a second grader?) is deliberately not stated; we do **not** say "two independent graders".

**Encapsulation**
- [ ] The holder is sonically welded around the coin, a permanent, tamper-evident seal. *Their site.*
- [ ] "We use the same sealing techniques and machinery as other third-party grading services." *Their site (pricing FAQ).*
- [ ] The holder protects the coin from handling while both sides stay in full view. *Standard practice.*
- [ ] Oversize holders for larger pieces. *Their site* (an extra).

**Labeling and certification**
- [ ] Label shows the attribution, the grade and a unique certificate number. *Standard practice; the label photo on their site shows grade, subgrades and a serial.*
- [ ] Every service includes a choice of ready-made designs for US, world, Roman and Greek coins. *Their site.* Custom designs for a collection. *Their site* (price removed).

**Registry record and verification**
- [ ] Every certificate number and the coin's details are entered in the certificate registry and can be checked on this site. *Depends on connecting his database (README).*

**Return shipping**
- [ ] Coins are packed and returned by **tracked** mail. *Standard practice.*
- [ ] "Signature delivery is required for orders valued over $750." *Their site* ("orders over $750"; is this the return shipment?). This is the only number left on the site.
- [ ] "We let you know when your coins are on their way." *Standard practice.*

## 3. The holder and label (/holder, home)

- [ ] Five parts: back shell, an insert that holds the coin **without touching its faces**, the coin, the label, and a clear front shell welded to the back. *Standard practice; the insert detail especially.*
- [ ] Once sealed it cannot be opened without visible damage. *Their site says "tamper-evident".*
- [ ] Protects from fingers, knocks and handling wear; both sides in full view. *Standard practice.*
- [ ] Tamper advice: cracks, seam separation or signs of opening, so contact us with the number before buying or selling.
- [ ] A scratched or damaged holder of ours can be reholdered; coins graded elsewhere can be crossed over. *Their site* (prices removed).
